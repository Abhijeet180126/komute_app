How Komüte works, in plain English

From signing up to arriving at your destination — here's every step, illustrated and broken down for both drivers and riders.

The complete journey — at a glance

Two paths, one platform. Drivers post trips, riders find them, and Komüte is the bridge in the middle that makes them connect.

Driver Journey

1

Create an account

Sign up with your name, email, mobile and password — or one tap with Google / Apple.

2

Verify your identity

Upload your driver's licence and a selfie. Our OCR + face match confirms it's really you — usually in seconds.

3

Add your vehicle

Make, model, year, plate, and a clear photo with the number plate visible.

4

Post a trip

From, to, date, time, seats, price. Add up to 5 stops along the way to pick up more passengers.

5

Wait for ride requests

Riders find your trip in search results. You'll see their requests in My Posted Rides.

Komüte connects you

Chat, agree a fair price, confirm the booking. We handle the matching — you handle the journey.

Rider Journey

1

Create an account

Same simple sign-up. As a rider you don't need a licence — just a profile so drivers know who they're meeting.

2

Search for a ride

Enter where you're starting, where you're going, and when. Komüte shows you every matching trip.

3

Pick a driver

Compare ratings, vehicles, and prices. Tap a trip to see the full route and driver profile.

4

Chat & agree on a price

Negotiate openly in the in-app chat — or book at the listed price if it works for you.

5

Hop in and travel

Meet at the agreed pickup spot. Sit back, enjoy the trip, then rate each other at the end.

Each step, in more detail

A closer look at the moments that matter most — from your first sign-up to your first review.

Step 1
Sign Up

Get on board in under a minute

Sign up takes about 60 seconds. You can use email and password, or skip the typing entirely with Google or Apple one-tap login.

  • No credit card. No subscriptions. Just an account.
  • You're 18+? You're in.
  • Pick whether you want to drive or ride — switch any time.
Step 2
Verification (Drivers Only)

Identity verified in seconds

Drivers upload a clear photo of their licence plus a selfie. Komüte's OCR reads the licence text, and our face match confirms the photo on the licence matches the selfie.

  • Most drivers are auto-approved in under 30 seconds.
  • Borderline matches go to a real human reviewer.
  • Riders see a green verified badge on every approved driver.
Step 3
Post or Search

Drivers post. Riders search. They meet in the middle.

Drivers post their trip — origin, destination, optional stops, departure time, available seats, and a starting price. Riders search for the same route on the same day, and matching trips show up instantly.

  • Up to 5 intermediate stops on one trip — pick up more riders along the way.
  • Recurring trips — set it up once for your weekly commute.
  • Smart Route Demand panel shows drivers which corridors are hot.
Step 4
Chat & Agree

Negotiate openly. No surge, no surprises.

Open chat with the driver, ask questions, and use the negotiation widget to propose a fair price. When you both agree, the booking confirms automatically.

  • A single passenger can chat with multiple drivers at once.
  • A driver can fill multiple seats from multiple chats simultaneously.
  • Counter-offers, accepts, declines — all tracked in the chat.
Step 5
Travel & Review

Hit the road. Then keep the trust loop going.

Meet at the pickup spot, share the trip, and arrive together. Once the ride is done, you both leave a quick rating and review — building each other's reputation for next time.

  • CO₂ savings calculated and added to your eco stats.
  • Star ratings keep the community safe and welcoming.
  • Frequent riders earn priority status with verified drivers.

Frequently asked questions

The short answers to the questions most people ask before their first ride.

Is Komüte a taxi? +
No — Komüte is a peer-to-peer carpooling platform. Drivers are everyday commuters and travellers offering empty seats on trips they were already taking. Komüte simply makes the introduction.
How is the price decided? +
The driver suggests a starting price when they post the trip. Riders can either book at that price, or open chat to negotiate. When both parties accept a price, the booking is confirmed automatically.
How does Komüte verify drivers? +
Drivers upload their driver's licence and a selfie during sign-up. Komüte runs OCR to read the licence and a face-match model to confirm the photo and the selfie are the same person. Verified drivers get a green badge.
Can I be both a driver and a rider? +
Yes — every account can switch roles at any time from the profile page. Many users are passengers during the week and drivers on weekends.
What if my trip is cancelled? +
Passengers who cancel more than 24 hours before departure get a full refund of the contribution. Cancellations under 24 hours forfeit the contribution. If the driver cancels, passengers always get a full refund.
Is Komüte available in my city? +
Komüte is launching in Canada — Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary, Ottawa, Edmonton — and expanding into the US through 2026 and 2027. Don't see your city yet? Sign up anyway and we'll let you know the moment we land.
