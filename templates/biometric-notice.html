To confirm you are the person on your driver's licence, Komüte compares a selfie you take now with the photo on your licence.

  • What we collect: a selfie from your camera, a photo of your licence that you add in the next step, and a face-match score worked out from them. The score is a measurement of your facial geometry, which some laws treat as biometric information.
  • Why: only to confirm your identity before you can offer rides. We don't use it for anything else, and we never sell it.
  • Who processes it: Komüte's own verification system, on our servers, only to produce this result. The images aren't sent to any other company.
  • How long we keep it: Komüte doesn't store the selfie or the licence photo: they are used for this check only and never saved. The face-match score is permanently deleted within 30 days of the verification decision. We keep only a pass or fail result, for up to 1 year.
  • Your choice: you don't have to agree. If you don't, you can still use Komüte as a rider, but you can't be verified as a driver. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@komute.net; we then delete any biometric data we still hold.
  • A person can review the result: the decision is made by an automated check. If it rejects you, you can ask a member of our team to review it from the result screen or your profile, or at privacy@komute.net. If the check can't reach a clear result, a person decides.

More detail: Privacy Policy, section 12.
