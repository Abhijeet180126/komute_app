Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: May 18, 2026

At KOMUTE, protecting your privacy and personal information is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and with whom we may share it. By using KOMUTE's services, you agree to the practices described here.

1
Information We Collect

To create an account and use KOMUTE, you may be asked to provide certain personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and other details needed to identify you. This information enables us to verify your account, communicate with you, and support your use of our service.

2
Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar tools to improve your experience with KOMUTE. Cookies help you stay logged in, remember preferences, and enable us to analyze usage patterns. You can choose to disable cookies, but doing so may limit some features of our platform.

3
How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us:

  • Verify your account and identity.
  • Provide and improve KOMUTE services.
  • Communicate important updates or service notices.
  • Analyze trends and enhance user experience.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties, except as described in this policy.

4
Legal and Safety Disclosures

KOMUTE may disclose your information:

  • If required by law or legal process.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of KOMUTE, our users, or the public.
  • For investigations of fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We will attempt to notify you of legal requests unless prohibited by law, court order, or risk of harm.

5
Log Files and Analytics

We automatically collect certain technical data (e.g., IP addresses and device information) to understand usage patterns and improve performance. This information does not directly identify you.

6
Service Announcements

We may send you service-related messages and updates about KOMUTE. These are considered part of using KOMUTE and cannot be opted out of.

7
Marketing Communications

If you receive marketing or promotional emails from us, you can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those messages.

8
Account Cancellation and Data Deletion

You may close your KOMUTE account at any time through your account settings or by contacting support. After account closure, your account will be deactivated and eventually removed from our system. You can also request deletion of your personal data in accordance with applicable laws.

9
Identity Verification

For safety and compliance, KOMUTE may use trusted third-party services to verify your identity. Biometric or ID data (if collected for verification) will only be used for verification purposes and handled in accordance with strict retention and deletion policies outlined by the verification provider.

10
Mobile App Information

If you use the KOMUTE mobile app, we may collect information permitted by app store policies (such as phone number or device data). We only share your phone number with other users when necessary to coordinate a confirmed trip for safety and communication.

11
Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will notify you via email or through the app so you are aware of what we collect and how it is used.
