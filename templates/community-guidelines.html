Community Guidelines

Komüte works because of trust. These guidelines keep every ride safe, respectful, and welcoming — for drivers and passengers alike.

Safety First

Verified drivers, honest profiles, and zero tolerance for unsafe behaviour.

Respect Everyone

Treat every traveller with kindness, courtesy, and fairness.

Be Reliable

Show up on time, honour your commitments, communicate clearly.

Travel Green

Share seats, cut emissions, and help build a cleaner commute.

A shared road, a shared responsibility

Komüte is a free community of everyday drivers and passengers helping each other get where they’re going. We don’t charge fees or commissions — we simply connect people. These guidelines apply to everyone, on every trip. They sit alongside our Terms & Conditions — breaking them can affect your rating or your access to the platform. Following them keeps Komüte a place people are glad to be part of.

1

Respect & Courtesy

Every person on Komüte deserves to feel safe and welcome, regardless of background, identity, or beliefs.

Please do

  • Greet your driver or passenger and confirm trip details.
  • Communicate respectfully in chat and in person.
  • Respect personal space, belongings, and quiet preferences.
  • Be patient and understanding if small delays happen.

Please don’t

  • Harass, insult, threaten, or discriminate against anyone.
  • Make unwanted advances or comments of a personal nature.
  • Use hate speech or offensive language.
  • Pressure others to change agreed plans or prices unfairly.
2

Safety on the Road

Safety is non-negotiable. These rules protect everyone in the vehicle.

For drivers

  • Hold a valid licence and insurance for every trip you post.
  • Keep your vehicle roadworthy — tyres, brakes, lights, seatbelts.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or any impairing substance.
  • Obey all traffic laws and posted speed limits.
  • Avoid using your phone while driving — pull over if you need to.

For passengers

  • Wear your seatbelt at all times.
  • Don’t distract the driver or interfere with the vehicle.
  • Never bring weapons, illegal substances, or hazardous items.
  • Speak up calmly if you ever feel unsafe — and end the trip if needed.
3

Honesty & Authentic Profiles

Trust on Komüte depends on people being who they say they are.

  • Use your real name, a clear photo, and accurate personal details.
  • Drivers must complete identity verification with genuine, unedited documents.
  • List accurate vehicle information — make, model, colour, and plate.
  • Post truthful trip details: real routes, times, seats, and starting prices.
  • Never create multiple or fake accounts, or impersonate another person.
4

Reliability & Communication

A carpool only works when everyone keeps their word.

  • Be on time at the agreed pickup point — for both pickup and departure.
  • Keep the in-app chat updated if plans change or you’re running late.
  • Cancel as early as possible if you genuinely cannot make a trip.
  • Avoid repeated last-minute cancellations — they affect your rating.
  • Honour the price you agreed on. Renegotiating at pickup is not okay.
5

Fair Cost-Sharing

Komüte is free to use. It’s about sharing the cost of a trip, not making a profit from passengers.

  • Any contribution should reflect a fair share of fuel and trip costs — never a commercial fare.
  • Agree the amount openly and respectfully through the in-app chat, before the trip.
  • Payment is arranged directly between driver and passenger — Komüte never charges fees or takes a cut.
  • Don’t use Komüte to run a commercial taxi or ride-hailing service.
6

What’s Not Allowed

The following behaviour can lead to immediate suspension or a permanent ban:

Zero tolerance

  • Violence, threats, or intimidation of any kind.
  • Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
  • Driving while impaired or recklessly.
  • Fraud, fake documents, or identity misrepresentation.

Also prohibited

  • Charging a commercial fare or profiting from trips.
  • Posting false listings or fake reviews.
  • Carrying weapons, drugs, or hazardous materials.
  • Transporting minors without consent and an adult present.
7

Ratings & Reviews

After every trip, drivers and passengers rate one another. Honest feedback keeps the community accountable.

  • Rate based on your genuine experience of the trip.
  • Keep written reviews factual, fair, and free of personal attacks.
  • Never post private information about another user in a review.
  • Don’t trade, inflate, or threaten to lower ratings to pressure someone.
8

How We Enforce These Guidelines

When a guideline is broken, we respond in proportion to what happened. Serious safety violations skip straight to removal.

1

Warning

For minor or first-time issues, we send a notice explaining what went wrong and what to change.

2

Temporary Suspension

Repeated or more serious issues lead to a temporary hold on posting trips or booking rides.

3

Permanent Removal

Severe violations — violence, fraud, impaired driving — result in a permanent ban from Komüte, and may be reported to authorities.

9

Emergencies & Reporting to Authorities

In an emergency, always contact local emergency services first — 911 in the United States and Canada, or 112 in Europe. In-app reporting is not monitored in real time and is not a substitute for emergency services.

  • Report non-emergency safety concerns to us through the app or our contact page so we can review the account involved.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and may disclose relevant account information in response to valid legal process, or where we believe in good faith it is necessary to prevent serious harm, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Serious incidents — violence, threats, fraud, or impaired driving — may be reported by us to the appropriate authorities.
