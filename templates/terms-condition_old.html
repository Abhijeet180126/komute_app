Nature of the Service

Komüte is a free peer-to-peer carpooling platform — a medium that connects drivers and passengers travelling in the same direction so they can share a trip together. Our purpose is to bring people together, reduce empty seats on the road, and make commuting greener and more social.

Komüte is free to use and is not a taxi service, a transportation network company, or a for-hire carrier. We charge no fees or commissions. Drivers are everyday commuters and travellers, not professional drivers, and are not employees, agents, or contractors of Komüte. Every trip is an independent arrangement between a driver and a passenger.

Komüte facilitates introductions and communication only. We do not provide transportation, do not process payments, and are not a party to any trip arranged through the Platform.

User Responsibilities

Drivers must

  • Hold a valid driver’s licence and required vehicle insurance throughout every trip.
  • Maintain their vehicle in safe, roadworthy operating condition.
  • Drive in compliance with all applicable traffic laws.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or any impairing substance.
  • Complete posted trips in good faith and cancel only when truly necessary.

Passengers must

  • Arrive on time at the agreed pickup point.
  • Honour any cost-sharing contribution agreed with the driver.
  • Behave respectfully toward the driver and any other passengers.
  • Not bring illegal substances, weapons, or hazardous items into the vehicle.

Cost-Sharing & Payments

Komüte is completely free to use. We do not charge booking fees, commissions, subscriptions, or any other fees — to drivers or to passengers — now or in the future as a condition of using the core platform.

  • Any contribution a passenger makes is agreed directly between the driver and the passenger, and may be discussed in the in-app chat before the trip.
  • Any agreed amount is a fair contribution toward the real cost of the trip — such as fuel — and is not a commercial fare. Drivers must not profit from trips.
  • Payment of any agreed contribution is arranged directly between the driver and the passenger. Komüte does not process, hold, or transfer money, and does not take any cut.
  • Because Komüte is a free connection service, we are not responsible for any payment, non-payment, or dispute over money exchanged between users.

Cancellations

Because Komüte does not charge fees or process payments, cancelling a trip on the Platform costs you nothing. We still ask everyone to cancel responsibly:

  • Cancel as early as possible when your plans change, so the other person has time to adjust.
  • Always let the other party know through the in-app chat when you cancel.
  • Drivers who cancel a posted trip should notify all confirmed passengers promptly.
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations may affect your rating and your continued access to the Platform.
  • Any contribution exchanged directly between users is settled between those users — Komüte is not involved.
