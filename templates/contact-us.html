Quick answers

You might find what you need right here.

How fast will I get a reply?

We aim to respond to all messages within one business day. Safety-related reports are prioritised and reviewed as soon as they arrive.

How do I report an unsafe driver or passenger?

Choose “Report a safety concern” as the topic in the form above, or email safety@komute.net directly. Every report is confidential and reviewed by our Trust & Safety team. For more on what we expect from users, see our Community Guidelines.

Is Komüte really free to use?

Yes — Komüte is completely free for both drivers and passengers. We don’t charge booking fees, commissions, or subscriptions. Komüte is simply a medium that connects people travelling the same way. Any fuel cost-sharing is agreed and settled directly between the driver and passenger.

I need help with a booking.

Select “Help with a booking” as your topic and include the trip details. Since Komüte is free and doesn’t process payments, any cost-sharing is arranged directly between you and the other traveller — see our Terms & Conditions for how cost-sharing works.

How do I become a verified driver?

Create an account, switch your role to Driver, and complete identity verification by uploading your driver’s licence and a selfie. Most drivers are verified within minutes.

Do you offer partnerships or press enquiries?

Absolutely. Email hello@komute.net or choose “Partnership / press” as your topic, and the right team will get back to you.
